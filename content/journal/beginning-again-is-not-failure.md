There is a poem by William Hickson called “Try Again”. Throughout the poem he encourages the reader to try again after a failed attempt. The one statement from the poem that has always stuck with me was “if at first you don’t succeed, try again”. Often times in life we set out to reach a goal and sometimes we do not accomplish it.

Have you ever watched a sports game? If so, you ever notice how when the team who does not win does not win, they do not quit and say oh well we lost that game so now we have to quit playing the sport. No, much like anything else in life they get up and they try again. Perhaps they win the next game, perhaps they lose, but one thing will remain true; that if they do not try again they have already failed.

There is a quote that I live by when I want to take a chance at something that may not yield results in my favor, and that quote says, “nothing beats a failure but a try”. I love it because if I do not try then my answer and results are already given, but if I do try then there is a chance that I can change the outcome of my circumstances.

So if you started college in your twenties and had to quit due to life circumstances, try again. If the first marriage did not work out, try again. If you have sent your resume out to so many jobs you lost count try again. If you went out for the team and almost made it, try again.

**It is never too late to start again.**

Will people have opinions about your start of course, but do not allow what others think determine if you go after what you want. Reasonably speaking you may be too old, or too young, or too whatever it is that is keeping you from going for the goal that you have in mind, but as long as you have breath in your lungs you can try again.

Life is full of people who have made choices that have required them to begin again. At some point we all have had to begin again in some way. Whether it was cooking the recipe that did not quite turn out right, driving to a location and getting off course, completing an assignment that was completely wrong, or simply starting over in life. Never stop trying.

Beginning again does not mean you failed. It means you lived. It means you learned some things the hard way, and by grace you still have breath in your body and courage in your heart to start where you are.

I know what it is to look at a chapter and wish you could rewrite it. You cannot go back, but you can begin again, wiser this time, softer in the right places and stronger in the places that matter. A new beginning rarely looks grand. Most days it looks like one small faithful choice, and then another.

So do not let shame name your next chapter. Let faith name it. You are not starting from nothing. You are starting from experience, and God is able to make even that fruitful.

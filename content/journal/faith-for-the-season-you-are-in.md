I have learned that faith is not only for the days when the answer is clear. Faith is for the mornings when you pray and still have to wait. It is for the hospital corridor and the quiet kitchen, for the moments when nobody sees you holding it together but God.

There were seasons when I wanted God to move quickly, and instead He taught me to stand firmly. Standing is still faith. Waiting is still faith. Getting up one more time and doing the next right thing, that is faith too.

If you are in a waiting season, I want you to hear this from my heart. God has not forgotten you. The silence is not absence. Keep praying, keep serving, keep believing, and watch how He meets you, one honest step at a time.

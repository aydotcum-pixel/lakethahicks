I wrote *Redemption: The Story of a Woman* because I knew I was not the only woman who had been broken by a season and wondered whether God could put her life back together. That first book is the story. It tells the truth about the breaking, and it tells the truth about the grace that met me there.

*Redemptions Journey* came two years later because I learned that being set free is only the beginning. After the rescue comes the walk. After the tears come the daily choices. The second book is for the woman who survived the hard chapter and now asks, Lord, how do I live free, day by day?

If my books do anything, I pray they sit with a woman the way a good friend would, honest and kind, and remind her that her story is not over. There is more to your story, and God is still writing.

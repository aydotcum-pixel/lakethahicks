I began writing in the fourth grade, which was the first time a teacher required me to write anything. It was an assignment and we were asked to write a poem about Thanksgiving. To my amazement the entire class seemed to be enjoying my poem and the praise that followed was overwhelming. Up until that point I had not written anything nor had the desire to. However, that moment sparked something in me and I would spend years writing skits, poems, and making cards for loved ones. In that entire process I had no idea that I had the ability or capacity to become an author.

More often than not one of the first questions people often ask is why I wrote Redemptions Journey? I often tell them I have no idea, because I honestly don’t remember the details of when I put pen to paper or shall I say keystrokes to the keyboard of my computer. I was in the process of going to school and working full time on 3rd shift, so the furthest thing from my mind was writing a book.

I can not tell you the why, but I can share the process and my journey in writing. I wrote what I thought was a finished product and shared with my siblings that I had written a book. Of course like all my other works and the great siblings they are, they were excited for me. Problem is I planned to tuck the writing away in my closet along with the pile of other poems and unfinished works I had done over the years.

If it was up to me no one would have ever read any books in the Redemption series. Thankfully, God had other plans and set in motion all the right people needed to help me complete the project and get it onto the market.

Book one was easy and seemed to flow with ease, when my publisher asked for more details on a character I simply prayed for revelation and God gave me a downpour. I did not plan for a book two, but my publisher asked if we could leave book one with a cliffhanger, and being the avid reader that I am, I said sure.

I had no idea that there was more to say, so much so that what I thought would be a one and done book became an entire three part series.

Books two and three required much more from me. These books required me to delve into my own life experiences to help me see things from the perspective of the characters. Book one garnered so much attention and the way it ministered into the lives of those who read it, I knew that the remaining books had to be just as powerful.

I have three goals for those who have or will read this series. Goal one is for you as a reader to not only read about the characters’ journey but to find something they said or experienced that draws you in and make you examine your own life. Goal two is to allow God to be seen and healing to take place in the lives of those who read the books. Goal three is that at the end of this three part series, everyone who reads it, found themselves, a friend, or loved one in the pages and are able to glean something of value from it to improve their lives.
